HOW IT PLAYS

Quick runs, fair deaths, and one more try every time.

JUMP, DOUBLE JUMP, SLASH

Tight, fair controls. Stomp on enemies or cut them down with your sword.

GIANT BOSSES

Every world ends with a boss that has its own tricks. Read its moves and find your opening.

20 HAND-MADE LEVELS

A world map, stars to earn, and traps in every corner: saws, darts, spikes and crumbling floors.

ENDLESS MODE

A new pit every run, plus a daily seed so you can compare scores.

INSTANT RETRY

No lives to lose. Die, restart, learn the level and go again.

MADE FOR TOUCH

On-screen controls for phones and tablets. Keyboard and gamepad work too. Plays offline.

KNOW YOUR THREATS
